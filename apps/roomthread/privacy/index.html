Roomthread — Privacy Policy

What the app stores, what it shares, and what it never does.

SupportPrivacyTermsAll apps

This policy describes what Roomthread version 1.0 does with data. It is written from the app's actual behaviour and is kept short on purpose.

What Roomthread stores and where

The hotels you save, the stays you log with their notes, dates and amounts, your ranking, your trip shortlists and the photos you attach are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own protected storage. Roomthread has no account and no Roomthread server keeps this information. The developer cannot see your journal.

Roomthread Plus (version 1.1 and later)

Roomthread Plus is an optional purchase with three helpers that use artificial intelligence. They are the only part of Roomthread that sends anything off your iPhone, and they do so only after you have agreed on the permission screen and only when you use them.

A stay from a confirmation: the text you paste is sent, after the app has removed e-mail addresses, phone numbers and long numbers such as booking, card and loyalty numbers. Names and addresses that remain in the text are sent with it.

Which one should I book: the hotels on that shortlist (name, city, country, official website), the trip's name, destination and dates, anything you type as a wish, and from your journal for each stay: hotel, city, year, reaction, trip purpose, room, standout, drawback, value and whether you would return. Private notes, exact dates of past stays, amounts paid and photos are never sent.

Your year in hotels: for each stay of the chosen year: hotel, city, country, month, reaction, trip purpose, room, standout, drawback and whether you would return, and your private notes only if you switch that on. Photos are never sent; they are added to the book on your iPhone. The finished book is kept on your iPhone until you write it again or erase the journal.

With each request the app also sends the App Store's signed record of your Plus purchase, so the service can check that the request comes from a purchased copy. That record does not contain your name, email address or Apple Account.

Where it goes: to the Roomthread Plus service, which the developer runs on servers of Supabase in the United States, and from there to OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C., United States), whose model writes the answer; for shortlist advice it also searches the web. The Plus service keeps none of the text: it passes the request on, returns the answer to your iPhone and stores only a usage counter under a one-way hash of your purchase's transaction number. Like any hosting provider, Supabase may keep technical request logs such as time and IP address under its own terms. OpenAI states that by default it does not use data sent through its API to train its models, and that it retains such data for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse.

Your control: without your agreement nothing is sent. Journal > Settings & privacy > Withdraw permission for Plus stops any further sending until you agree again. Answers are written by AI and can be wrong; everything Plus fills in stays editable.

What the developer collects

Roomthread contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not send your journal anywhere. Version 1.0 collects nothing (App Store label “Data Not Collected”). From version 1.1, if you use Plus, the App Store label lists “Other User Content” and “Purchase History”, both used only to make Plus work and shown as linked to you because each request carries your purchase record; the developer keeps none of the content. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and what you write, and uses it only to help you.

Maps and links

Hotel locations are shown with Apple Maps, which loads map tiles from Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. Links to a hotel's official website, and to this site, open in your browser; those sites have their own policies.

Photos

You choose photos through the system photo picker, so Roomthread only receives the pictures you pick and never has access to your library. The app keeps a resized copy with its location metadata removed. The copies stay on your iPhone.

Backups

Your journal is part of your own iPhone backup, on your computer or in your iCloud account, under your Apple Account. The developer has no access to those backups.

Exporting and deleting your data

Journal → Settings & privacy → Export my data creates a file of your journal. Journal → Settings & privacy → Erase this journal removes every stay, saved hotel, ranking, shortlist and photo from the app. Deleting the app removes the journal from the iPhone.

Children

Roomthread is a general travel journal and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

This policy is updated when the app's data practices change, for example if a later version adds optional accounts. The date at the bottom of the page is the current version, and a change of that kind would be described here before it ships.
