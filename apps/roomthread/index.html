About Roomthread

Roomthread is a private hotel journal for iPhone. Log the hotels you have stayed in, keep the details you want to remember, put your stays in your own order, save hotels for later and build a shortlist of up to five options for your next trip. Version 1.0 keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no account.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Version 1.0 has no accounts, no sign-up and no feed. Open the app and start logging.

Where is my journal stored?

On your iPhone, in the app's own protected storage. It is not uploaded anywhere and the developer cannot see it. It is included in your own iPhone backup, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings the journal with it.

Which hotels are in the catalog?

A starting set of 117 sourced hotels in New York, London, Paris, Rome, Lisbon, Munich, Tokyo, Hong Kong, Shanghai and Sydney. The coverage is deliberately small. If your hotel is missing, choose Add a missing hotel in Explore and it becomes a private entry in your journal.

Does Roomthread show prices, availability or reviews?

No. Roomthread does not sell rooms and shows no live prices, availability or third-party review scores. Choosing a hotel on a shortlist is a planning note, not a booking. Check details with the hotel before you travel.

How does the ranking work?

The order is yours. When you log a stay you can compare the hotel with past stays to place it, leave it unranked, or drag it later in Your ranking. It is a personal order, not a quality score.

What is Roomthread Plus? (version 1.1 and later)

An optional one-time purchase with three helpers that use AI: a stay from a pasted booking confirmation; advice on a shortlist (“Which one should I book?”) from what your own journal says matters to you, with a source for every fact about the hotels; and “Your year in hotels”, a small book from your entries with your own photos. Plus never shows prices, availability or ratings and books nothing. The purchase includes up to 60 requests and 10 answers with web search in any 30 days, 200 and 30 in total, and is shared with your Family Sharing group. Everything else stays free.

What does Plus send, and to whom?

Only when you use Plus and have agreed on the permission screen: a pasted confirmation (the app first removes e-mail addresses, phone numbers and booking and card numbers) or a shortlist with a summary of your journal is sent to the Roomthread Plus service and on to OpenAI. Private notes (unless you include them in the year book), amounts paid and photos are never sent. The Plus service keeps none of it. The details are in the privacy policy; you can withdraw the permission under Journal → Settings & privacy → “Withdraw permission for Plus”.

Plus says it isn’t available right now.

The Plus service can’t be reached for the moment. Your journal is untouched and nothing is taken from your allowance. Try again later; the rest of the app keeps working without the internet.

What is “Each year at a glance”? (version 1.2.1 and later)

A summary in the Journal of each year you have logged: how many stays and hotels, which cities, how many you loved and how many you would go back to. It is counted on your iPhone from your journal, without AI, for every journal, with or without Plus. Stays without a year are not included.

Can I add photos?

Yes, up to six per stay. Roomthread resizes the copies it keeps and removes their location metadata. They stay on your iPhone.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Journal → Settings & privacy → Export my data creates a file with your hotels, stays, rankings and shortlists that you can save or share. Photos stay in the app and are not part of the file.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a stay, a saved hotel or a shortlist where you see it, or use Journal → Settings & privacy → Erase this journal to remove everything at once. Deleting the app also removes the journal from the iPhone.

Is Roomthread free?

Yes. The journal, the hotel catalog, your ranking and your shortlists are free, with no subscriptions or advertising. Plus (version 1.1 and later) is an optional one-time purchase.
