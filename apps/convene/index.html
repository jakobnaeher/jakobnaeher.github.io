About WhenWeGo

WhenWeGo is a group trip planner for iPhone and iPad. Each person enters their own free dates, departure city, budget and preferences; WhenWeGo finds the dates that work, compares destinations, keeps one shared plan for stays, travel and the day-by-day itinerary, and lets the group decide through proposals and votes. An optional assistant runs on the device (Apple Intelligence) and can only propose — people accept or reject. From version 1.1 an optional Planner uses AI and web search to suggest real places to stay, routes, a day-by-day plan and destinations.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. WhenWeGo uses your iCloud account (the one your iPhone is signed into). There is no WhenWeGo account, password or sign-up.

How do I invite people?

Open the trip and tap Invite. WhenWeGo uses iCloud sharing, so you can send the invitation by Messages, Mail or a link. Invited people need to be signed into iCloud on their device.

What can an invited person see and change?

Everyone on a trip sees the shared plan: trip name, dates, destinations, proposals and votes, stays, travel legs, itinerary, shared expenses, and each person's name, departure city, interests, free/blocked date ranges, travel limits and budget level (1–5). Each person edits only their own details, travel and stays; the organizer can edit everything, is the only one who chooses dates and destinations or accepts proposals, and the only one who can remove people. Read-only invitations are available too.

Is my budget visible to the group?

Your exact budget figure is stored in your own iCloud and is never put into the shared trip unless you turn on “Share my budget figure” yourself. The group sees a budget level (1–5) so destinations can be compared.

What is the Planner? (version 1.1 and later)

An optional one-time purchase. From a trip, the Planner can suggest real places to stay that fit the group (each with the web page it was found on), the best route for each traveller with flight searches already filled in, a day-by-day plan the group votes on, and destination ideas that work for everyone. What you like is added to the trip as a proposal: a stay without a price, a travel leg without times, day plans under Proposals. The purchase includes 40 requests in any 30 days and 120 in total, and can be shared through Family Sharing. Everything else in WhenWeGo stays free.

Does the Planner know prices or availability?

No, and it never pretends to. It names places and routes and gives its source; for prices, times and availability it opens a live search on the provider’s site with your dates filled in. Its suggestions are written by AI from web pages and can be wrong or out of date, so open the source before you rely on one.

What does the Planner send, and to whom?

After you agree on its permission screen, and only when you ask it for something, a description of the trip without names is sent to the WhenWeGo planner service and on to OpenAI, whose AI searches the web and writes the suggestions. People are sent as “Traveller 1”, “Traveller 2” with home city, limits, interests, budget level and accommodation and access needs. The planner service keeps none of it. The details are in the privacy policy, and you can withdraw the permission in Trip settings.

Does WhenWeGo book flights or hotels?

No. WhenWeGo helps you plan and then hands off to booking sites with your dates and cities prefilled. Prices you type in are shown as entered by you, with the date you entered them. WhenWeGo never shows live fares or availability and never marks anything as booked on its own — you mark a stay or flight as booked after you have booked it with the provider.

Where does the weather information come from?

From Open-Meteo's historical reanalysis data: WhenWeGo averages the last five complete years for the month of your trip and labels it as a recent average — not a forecast. Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0.

Where do exchange rates come from?

European Central Bank euro reference rates via the Frankfurter API. They are indicative planning rates, usually updated once per working day, not the rate your card or bank will use. WhenWeGo shows the rate date and when it last retrieved them.

Does the assistant send my trip to an AI company?

No. (The optional Planner is a different feature and does, after you agree; see above.) The assistant uses Apple Intelligence on your device (iOS 26 and later, on supported devices). Nothing you type to it leaves your device. On devices without Apple Intelligence the assistant explains that it isn't available; every other planning feature works.

What does “Still open” on a trip show? (version 1.3 and later)

What the group still has to enter: who has not said when they can travel, a home city or a budget level, and whether there are destination ideas; once dates and a destination are chosen, who has no journey yet, who has nights without a stay, and which stays and journeys have no price. WhenWeGo works this out from the trip itself, without AI, on every iPhone. The assistant’s “What’s missing?” gives the same answer.

Can I trust the assistant’s answers?

They are written by Apple’s model on your iPhone and can be wrong, so check them against the trip. “What’s missing?” is worked out by the app itself and is exact. The assistant only suggests; nothing changes until someone accepts it. From version 1.4, when an answer talks about a stay or a journey, the trip’s own details for it (price, dates, stops, as entered) are shown right under the answer, marked as coming from the trip; where the assistant differs, the trip is right.

Can WhenWeGo fill in a journey from a booking e-mail? (version 1.4 and later)

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, yes. Under Stays & travel, tap Add travel and paste the confirmation into “From a confirmation”, then tap “Fill in from it”. Apple’s on-device model reads it; nothing is sent. Only what the text shows is filled in (from, to, dates and times as printed, stops, price), and the form says what it left out. Check everything before you save.

How do I leave or delete a trip?

Trip settings → “Manage or leave this trip” for a trip you were invited to; “Delete trip” for a trip you organize (this deletes it for everyone it is shared with). When you confirm leaving, your access ends and the private budget figure you entered for that trip is deleted from your iCloud. Your name, dates, stays and travel stay in the organizer's copy of the plan until the organizer removes you under People — ask them if you want that gone too.

Can I export the plan?

Yes: share it as text or PDF from the trip screen at any time.
