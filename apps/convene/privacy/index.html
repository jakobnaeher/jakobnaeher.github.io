WhenWeGo — Privacy Policy

What the app stores, what it shares, and what it never does.

SupportPrivacyAll apps

This policy describes what WhenWeGo does with data. It is written from the app's actual behaviour and is kept short on purpose.

What WhenWeGo stores and where

Trips, participants and plan data are stored on your devices and in iCloud using Apple's CloudKit: a trip you organize lives in your private iCloud database; when you share it, its contents are placed in a shared CloudKit zone that the people you invite can read (and, with edit access, change). Your exact budget figure is stored only in your private iCloud database and only published to the shared trip if you choose to share it. WhenWeGo has no server of its own and the developer cannot access your trips.

What is shared with the group

On a shared trip, everyone with access sees: trip name, dates, destinations, proposals and votes, stays, travel legs, itinerary items, shared expenses, and for each participant their name, departure city and airports, interests, free and blocked date ranges, flexibility, travel-time and connection limits, accessibility notes, lodging preference and budget level (1–5). Share trips with people you trust: iCloud sharing grants read or read-write access to the whole trip, and the app's finer rules (who may edit what) are enforced by the app, not by the server.

Network requests

WhenWeGo makes two kinds of keyless requests to third-party services, neither of which includes your name, identity, budget or dates: (1) a destination's coordinates and past date ranges to Open-Meteo (archive-api.open-meteo.com) to compute typical weather; (2) a request for the current euro rate table to Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). Like any web request, these reveal your IP address to the provider, who may keep technical logs for a limited time under their own terms. Place search uses Apple Maps (MapKit) under Apple's terms. Invitation and sync traffic goes through Apple's iCloud.

Assistant

The optional assistant runs on your device with Apple's Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence). No prompt or trip data is sent to WhenWeGo or to any AI provider.

What the developer collects

Nothing. WhenWeGo contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and has no server. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. If you email support, the developer receives your email and what you write, and uses it only to help you.

Permissions

WhenWeGo asks for no device permissions beyond iCloud (used for storage and sharing). It does not access your calendar, contacts, photos or location.

Deleting your data

Delete a trip you organize to remove it for everyone (Trip settings → Delete trip). Leave a trip you were invited to from Trip settings; your participant entry is removed from the shared trip and your private budget record is removed from your iCloud once the trip is gone from your device. Deleting the app removes its local data; iCloud data is removed when you delete your trips or manage WhenWeGo's data in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.

Children

WhenWeGo is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for general audiences.

Changes

This policy is updated when the app's data practices change; the date at the bottom of the page is the current version.
