About Rows

Rows keeps your place in a pattern: import a PDF or photo, drag the row marker to the line you are on, highlight repeats, and count rows with big counters that roll into repeat counters. Projects sync between your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Rows uses your iCloud account for sync. There is no Rows account, no sign-up and no subscription.

Where are my patterns stored?

On your devices and in your private iCloud (CloudKit). They are never uploaded to any Rows server — there isn't one — and never shared with anyone. Rows does not re-host or redistribute patterns.

What is free and what is the unlock?

Free: two projects and one pattern per project, with full export. A single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited projects and patterns. No subscription, ever.

I bought the unlock on my iPhone; my iPad says I haven't.

Open Settings → Restore purchase on the iPad. The unlock is tied to your Apple Account, so every device signed in with it can restore.

How does undo work with linked counters?

Undo reverses exactly what your last tap did — including the rollover it caused on the linked repeat counter — and nothing else. If you also tapped the repeat counter directly in between, that change stays.

Can I back everything up or move to a new device?

Yes. Settings → Export everything gives you a folder with every project as JSON plus every pattern file exactly as you imported it. Import the folder's rows.json on the new device. Projects already present on a device are left unchanged.

Does the row marker move by itself when I count?

No. A PDF has no idea where its rows are, so Rows never guesses: you drag the marker, Rows counts. The marker stays exactly where you put it.

Apple Watch?

Not in the first version. It is planned, and the counter design is prepared for it.
