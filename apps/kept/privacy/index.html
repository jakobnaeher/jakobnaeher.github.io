In Their Words — Privacy Policy

What the app stores, what it shares, and what it never does.

SupportPrivacyAll apps

This policy describes what In Their Words does with data. It is written from the app's actual behaviour and is kept short on purpose.

What In Their Words stores and where

Storytellers (name, relationship, year of birth, portrait), recordings, transcripts, photos you add, your own questions and which deck questions were asked are stored on your device and in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Only devices signed in with your Apple Account can access them. The developer cannot access your recordings.

The storybook and other AI features (version 1.1 and later)

The storybook turns your stories into written chapters and painted pictures with the help of artificial intelligence. From version 1.2 the book can also be drawn as comic pages and translated into another language, a story can suggest follow-up questions to ask next, and you can ask a question about what was said and get an answer with the passages it rests on. These AI features are the only ones that send anything off your device, and they do so only after you have agreed on the permission screen and only when you ask for a book to be written, updated or translated, for follow-up questions, or put a question to the stories.

What is sent: the text of the stories being written (the transcript and its question), the storyteller’s name, relationship and year of birth, the language, and your choices of style. For a picture, a one-sentence description of a scene from the story. For a translation, the written chapters of the book and the language to translate into. For follow-up questions, the transcript of that one story and its question. For a question to the stories, your question and the transcripts of that storyteller’s stories with their questions (as many as fit into one request; the app tells you how many were read). With each request the app also sends the App Store’s signed record of your one-time unlock, so the service can check that the request comes from a purchased copy.

What is never sent: recordings (audio), photos and portraits.

Where it goes: to the In Their Words book service, which the developer runs on servers of Supabase in the United States, and from there to OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C., United States), whose models do the writing and painting. The book service keeps no story text and no pictures: it passes the request on, returns the result to your device and stores only a usage counter under a one-way hash of your purchase’s transaction number. Like any hosting provider, Supabase may keep technical request logs such as time and IP address under its own terms. OpenAI states that by default it does not use data sent through its API to train its models, and that it retains such data for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse.

Where the book is kept: the finished chapters, pictures and translations are stored on your device only. A follow-up question you keep is stored with your own questions, on your devices and in your private iCloud. They are removed when you delete the book, the storyteller, or everything.

Your control: without your agreement nothing is sent. Settings → Withdraw permission for the storybook stops any further sending until you agree again. AI can make mistakes; every chapter can be corrected by hand, and the recordings and word-for-word transcripts are never changed by the storybook.

Microphone and speech recognition

In Their Words uses the microphone only while you are recording a story, and speech recognition only to transcribe your recordings on the device. Transcription runs on the device (Apple's on-device speech recognition); In Their Words never sends audio to In Their Words or to any speech service. On iOS 26 and later the system may download a speech language model from Apple once.

Photos and notifications

Photos are added through the system photo picker; In Their Words receives only the photos you choose. The optional weekly reminder is a local notification on your device — In Their Words sends no email and no push notifications from a server.

What the developer collects

In Their Words contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Apart from the storybook described above, the app makes no network requests of its own; sync is handled by Apple's iCloud and purchases by Apple's App Store. Because of the storybook, the App Store privacy label lists “Other User Content” and “Purchase History”, both used only to make the feature work. The label shows them as linked to you, because stories contain names and personal details and each request carries the transaction record of your purchase. The developer keeps no story text and has no account, name or email address for you. Nothing is used for tracking or advertising. If you email support, the developer receives your email and what you write, and uses it only to help you.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store and can be shared through Family Sharing. In Their Words checks entitlements with StoreKit on your device. The developer receives no personal information about you from a purchase; the storybook sends the App Store’s signed transaction record to the book service as described above, and that record does not contain your name, email address or Apple Account.

Other people's voices

You are responsible for recording other people only with their agreement. In Their Words asks you to confirm this before the first recording with each storyteller and makes deleting a story or a whole storyteller easy.

Deleting your data

Delete a story or a storyteller to remove the recordings, transcripts and photos from all your devices and your iCloud; Settings → Delete everything removes all In Their Words data, including storybooks on the device. You can also manage In Their Words's iCloud data in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.

Children

In Their Words is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for general audiences.

Changes

This policy is updated when the app's data practices change; the date at the bottom of the page is the current version.
