Equant — Methodology

Pre-release information · Updated October 6, 2026

Pre-release information. Equant is being prepared for the App Store. Its public research service is not available yet. These pages describe the current app implementation and planned first release; they do not announce a launch date.

Current model: 1.6.0

This is a technical summary of the model in the current source. It is not evidence of a deployed data feed or a validated investment outcome. A rating snapshot carries the model version that produced it; a future release may change the model and update this page.

Combined score

Stocks combine valuation and analyst components as follows:

combinedScore = 0.60 × valuationScore + 0.40 × analystScore

For equity ETFs, the normal blend is:

combinedScore = 0.70 × valuationScore + 0.30 × analyst/fundScore

Each component uses a 0–100 scale. When ETF analyst/fund inputs are unavailable, the model can use valuation alone and limits confidence according to holdings coverage and freshness.

Valuation and peer groups

The model compares forward price/earnings values with a peer group, combining relative discount and percentile position. Peer selection considers industry, geography, market-cap group and, when supplied, liquidity. It excludes a valuation signal when required inputs are missing or unsuitable, and reduces sensitivity to percentile position when the peer distribution is very narrow.

Analyst component and confidence

The analyst component combines provider-supplied rating buckets. Coverage, age of inputs, peer-group quality, estimate dispersion and ETF holdings coverage affect confidence. Low confidence restricts extreme rating labels; insufficient inputs produce “Insufficient Data”.

Rating bands

80 or above: Strong Buy
65 to below 80: Buy
45 to below 65: Neutral
30 to below 45: Sell
Below 30: Strong Sell

These labels are formula outputs. They do not account for a person's finances, and a label is not a forecast or a tailored recommendation.

Limitations

Input data may be stale, incomplete or wrong. A forward P/E comparison can be unsuitable for some industries or companies with invalid earnings inputs. The model includes confidence limits and explanatory notes for such cases, but these do not remove investment risk. No production dataset, licensed provider coverage or historical performance is claimed by this page.
