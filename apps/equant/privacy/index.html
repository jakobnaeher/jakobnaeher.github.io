Equant — Privacy Policy

Pre-release information · Updated October 6, 2026

Pre-release information. Equant is being prepared for the App Store. Its public research service is not available yet. These pages describe the current app implementation and planned first release; they do not announce a launch date.

Scope

This page describes the current development app and the planned first release. The public research service is not live. Provider configuration, service retention and deletion verification will be reflected here before the app is made generally available.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded media or contact forms. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement. Those hosting logs are separate from Equant app data.

Optional Apple account

The app can be used without an Equant account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, an Apple identity token is passed to Supabase to create or resume an account. Apple may provide an email address, including a private relay address when you choose Hide My Email. Supabase issues an account identifier and session credentials. The app requests Apple's email scope, not full name. Phone and SMS sign-in are disabled in the current first-release configuration.

Data stored on your device

The current watchlist, recent-search list, cached research data and notification preferences are stored on the device. The app also creates a random installation identifier. The current interface sends that identifier with an account-deletion request; it does not upload the local watchlist as a stored portfolio.

Research requests

Search text, individual ticker symbols and sets of watchlist ticker symbols are sent to the Equant research service when looking up or refreshing public research data. A local recent-search list is not uploaded as a list. The current client does not attach its account session token to ordinary research reads. Network operators can still process IP addresses and connection information, so this does not make a request anonymous to every service involved.

Operational information

The backend implementation logs request method, endpoint path, response status, elapsed time and request identifier. It removes query strings from this completion log; an asset endpoint path can still contain its ticker. Protected account actions may include an account identifier. Deletion-completion logs can also include the installation identifier, deletion reason and status. The service uses IP addresses for routing and rate limiting. Authentication and hosting providers may retain their own security and diagnostic records.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any details you choose to include. The app's feedback composer can include a category, ticker, app version, iOS version and device model. You decide whether to send the message through your email provider.

Retention and deletion

Local watchlist and recent-search entries stay on the device until you remove or clear them. The account-deletion flow asks the service to revoke the Apple sign-in connection where needed and remove the optional account. After confirmed completion, the app clears the local watchlist and recent-search list. Deletion of an account does not establish that all security or operational logs have been erased.

No request-log deletion period or provider retention guarantee has been verified for this unreleased service. The final service policy and its implementation will be stated here before general availability. This page does not promise automatic deletion after 30 days.

For an access, correction or deletion request, contact jago.naeher@gmail.com. Do not send account passwords or one-time codes.
