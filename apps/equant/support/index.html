Equant — Support

Pre-release information · Updated October 6, 2026

Pre-release information. Equant is being prepared for the App Store. Its public research service is not available yet. These pages describe the current app implementation and planned first release; they do not announce a launch date.

Contact

For Equant questions, a bug report, or a request about your data, email jago.naeher@gmail.com. You can include the app version, iOS version, device model and, for a data question, the ticker symbol. Do not include passwords or sign-in codes.

Accounts

The current app offers Continue without an account and optional Sign in with Apple. Phone and SMS sign-in are not enabled for the planned first release. A working production service and real account flows are still being verified.

Watchlists and searches

The current app stores the watchlist and recent-search list on the device. Research requests still send search text and ticker symbols to the research service when refreshing data. The app does not connect to brokerage accounts.

Deleting an optional account

The app includes Settings → Account → Delete my account. This requests deletion from the service and may ask you to confirm with Apple again. Local watchlist and recent-search data are cleared after the service confirms account deletion. A network error or incomplete response is not confirmation that deletion finished.

If you cannot complete a deletion request, contact the address above. The production deletion flow remains under verification.

Ratings and methodology

The methodology page explains the current model. Pre-release sample data is not proof of a live provider feed, and a rating is not a prediction of investment returns.
