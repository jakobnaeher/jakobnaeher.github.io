Pre-release information. Equant is being prepared for the App Store. Its public research service is not available yet. These pages describe the current app implementation and planned first release; they do not announce a launch date.

Research information

Equant's planned app presents model-based research computed uniformly from data inputs. It does not assess your financial circumstances, objectives, risk tolerance or time horizon. Research data and ratings may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate; model output does not predict investment returns.

The current app does not execute trades, hold funds or connect to brokerage accounts. The information on this website is a description of the app and its model, not an instruction to buy or sell an investment.

Availability

The public app and research service have not launched. This website does not promise service availability, a launch date or access to any particular data feed. Production provider permissions and service verification are still being completed.

Using the service

Use the app through its intended interface. Do not bypass access controls, interfere with the service, or copy and redistribute data in bulk without the relevant rights. Any provider restrictions disclosed with a future release must be respected.

Accounts and privacy

The current app offers guest access and optional Sign in with Apple. Phone sign-in is disabled. See the privacy page for current data flows and the account-deletion request path.

App distribution terms

When an App Store version becomes available, consult its listing for the applicable app licence terms. This page will be updated for that release.
