Pre-release information. Equant is being prepared for the App Store. Its public research service is not available yet. These pages describe the current app implementation and planned first release; they do not announce a launch date.

About Equant

Equant is an iPhone research app for comparing publicly traded stocks and equity ETFs with a common scoring model. The planned app includes a local watchlist, search, rating history, and notes about data freshness and confidence.

The model applies the same formula to the same inputs for every user. It does not use a person's financial circumstances to tailor a rating. Production data feeds and service verification are still being completed.

No live market data or investment results are published on this website.
